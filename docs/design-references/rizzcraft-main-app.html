Why this reply works.
A quick read on the moves behind the words. Use it once, internalise it forever.
Your reply
"Bold claim. I'm going to need evidence — Friday?"
1
It matches her energy
She was playful and a bit cheeky. Anything serious or eager would break the rhythm she set.
2
It's specific, not generic
"Evidence" calls back her exact claim. References beat compliments — they prove you were listening.
3
The ask hides inside the joke
"— Friday?" is a low-pressure invite. If she's not into it, she can sidestep without it feeling like a rejection.
4
It's short
Nine words. Confident texts don't over-explain. Length signals overthinking — brevity signals ease.
